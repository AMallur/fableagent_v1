# Security Awareness and Training Policy

**Citation:** 45 CFR §164.308(a)(5) — implement a security awareness and
training program for all workforce members.

**Owner:** HIPAA Security Officer. **Review cycle:** annually.

## 1. Policy

1. **Initial training.** Every workforce member with access to any system
   in scope of the Access Control Policy completes a HIPAA security
   awareness training course before being granted access to production
   systems, and no later than 30 days after any onboarding exception.
2. **Content.** Training covers, at minimum: what constitutes PHI, the
   minimum-necessary standard, this Company's Access Control and Encryption
   policies, how to recognize and report a suspected security incident, and
   the consequences described in the Workforce Sanction Policy.
3. **Refresher cadence.** Training is repeated at least annually, and
   immediately after any material policy change or after any workforce
   member is involved in a Tier 2+ sanction event.
4. **Recordkeeping.** Completion is logged in
   `compliance/workforce_training_log.csv` with the workforce member's
   name, training source/course name, completion date, and (if issued) a
   certificate reference. Records are retained at least 6 years per
   §164.316(b)(2)(i).
5. **Acceptable training sources.** Any HIPAA security-awareness course from
   a reputable provider satisfies this policy (examples: HHS/OCR's own
   published training materials, or a paid course from a provider such as
   MedTrainer, Compliancy Group, or a bundled course included with a
   compliance-automation platform like Vanta/Drata/Secureframe). The
   Security Officer selects the source and records it in the log.
