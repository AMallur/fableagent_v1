## How to use this package

- These documents are structured implementation drafts. They must be customized with named officers, production vendors, executed agreements, system configuration, customer requirements and evidence before formal approval. They do not constitute legal advice, a government certification, or proof that untested production controls operate effectively.
- Complete all bracketed required fields and resolve every Conditional, Required, Open or TBD status.
- Have the Privacy Officer, Security Officer, Engineering Owner and healthcare counsel review the applicable documents.
- Map policies and standards to the production configuration, tests, tickets and evidence repository.
- Approve documents through a dated management process and train affected workforce members.
- Review periodically and after material changes, incidents, new vendors, new PHI uses or legal changes.
